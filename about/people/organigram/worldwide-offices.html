Worldwide Offices

Stretching from the most isolated islands in the heart of the Pacific, across the vast and varied landscapes of Central Asia and Africa, and reaching the western shores of South America, AM works where innovation and economy start from a small window of opportunity. That is, in the most remote regions of developing countries and fragile situations.

Spanning five regions and 92 countries, we reach those who are otherwise left behind by development projects. Often called ‘the last mile’, we at AM consider this the first mile, at which small-scale enterprises grow their economy that nourishes the world.
