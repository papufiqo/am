History

In the last 36 months, several major banks and investment firms are playing a crucial role in fueling the growth of startups. These institutions provide not only financial backing but also strategic guidance and mentorship to entrepreneurs. Some of the prominent players with which we have been partnering, include: -Merak Capital: An investment firm focused on early-stage technology startups. Merak Capital has been instrumental in identifying and nurturing high-potential startups, providing them with the necessary resources to succeed. -Shorooq Partners: A leading venture capital firm in the Middle East, Shorooq Partners has made significant investments in Saudi startups, particularly in the fintech and tech sectors. Their support has been vital in accelerating the growth of these startups. -Saudi Venture Capital: A government-backed initiative aimed at supporting the startup ecosystem through direct investments and co-investment programs. Saudi Venture Capital has played a pivotal role in bridging the funding gap for early-stage startups. We have been part of the innovation evaluation process, for each of them.

We have also been valuable partners in the Saudi National Technology Development Program (NTDP), which is a cornerstone of Saudi Arabia’s efforts to foster technological innovation. The NTDP aims to support research and development activities, promote collaboration between academia and industry, and accelerate the commercialization of new technologies. Key components of the NTDP that we included in our policy are: -Funding for R&D Projects: Providing grants and financial support for research projects in strategic areas such as artificial intelligence, biotechnology, and renewable energy. -Partnerships and Collaborations: Encouraging partnerships between universities, research institutions, and private companies to drive innovation and knowledge transfer. -Infrastructure Development: Investing in state-of-the-art research facilities and technology parks to create an environment conducive to innovation.
